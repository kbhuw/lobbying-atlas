## Center for Sportfishing Policy

- **Exact website:** https://sportfishingpolicy.com/
- **Plain description:** A membership advocacy organization that brings marine recreational fishing and boating stakeholders together to shape federal fisheries policy and promote stewardship of marine resources.
- **Type / ownership evidence:** The organization’s FAQ says it was formerly the Center for Coastal Conservation, was organized by individuals and organizations, and is a nonprofit under IRC section 501(c)(4); its About page says it is a membership organization overseen by a board of directors. There is no owner/shareholder evidence because this is a membership nonprofit.
- **Sources:** https://sportfishingpolicy.com/about/frequent-questions/ (former name, incorporation/history, 501(c)(4), management); https://sportfishingpolicy.com/about/ (mission, membership and board governance); https://sportfishingpolicy.com/category/archives/ (official 2016 rename archive).
- **Suitable official logo:** https://sportfishingpolicy.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/vector.png (logo asset on the organization’s WordPress site).

## Center for Transportation and the Environment (CTE)

- **Exact website:** https://cte.tv/
- **Plain description:** A member-supported nonprofit that develops, deploys and helps commercialize zero-emission vehicles and supporting transportation infrastructure, with emphasis on medium- and heavy-duty fleets.
- **Type / ownership evidence:** CTE’s official Mission & Impact page explicitly identifies it as a member-supported 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization; no private owner or equity ownership is claimed.
- **Sources:** https://cte.tv/mission (501(c)(3) status, mission, services and project focus); https://cte.tv/ (official homepage/domain).

## Center Road Solutions LLC

- **Exact website:** https://www.centerroadsolutions.com/
- **Plain description:** A Washington, D.C.-based boutique government-relations and public-policy firm that develops legislation, secures federal funding, conducts regulatory advocacy, and builds coalitions for nonprofits, foundations and socially responsible businesses.
- **Type / ownership evidence:** The current official site describes the firm as a “woman-owned” bipartisan boutique firm. An official U.S. LDA filing identifies the registrant as “Center Road Solutions” and lists its D.C. address and domain contact; the D.C. government business-report list records the legal entity as “Center Road Solutions L.L.C.” The sources support an LLC and woman-owned status, but do not establish a particular individual’s percentage ownership.
- **Sources:** https://www.centerroadsolutions.com/ (description, woman-owned claim, work and D.C. location); https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/65ba63b5-3650-4770-ab32-f32e72267bee/print/ (official lobbying filing, registrant/contact/domain); https://dlcp.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/DLCP/publication/attachments/2018%20List%20of%20All%20Entities%20Required%20to%20File%20Biennial%20Report_1.pdf (D.C. list entry for Center Road Solutions L.L.C.).

## Centerra Gold Inc.

- **Exact website:** https://www.centerragold.com/
- **Plain description:** A Canadian-based gold and copper mining company headquartered in Toronto, focused on exploration, development, operation and acquisition of mining assets in Canada, the United States and Türkiye.
- **Type / ownership evidence:** Centerra is a publicly traded Canadian corporation. Its official investor page says its shares trade on the Toronto Stock Exchange under **CG** and the New York Stock Exchange under **CGAU**. This supports public/equity ownership; the official site does not claim a controlling private owner in the reviewed pages.
- **Sources:** https://www.centerragold.com/overview/default.aspx (company description, headquarters and operating assets); https://www.centerragold.com/investor-relations/investor-overview/default.aspx (public listing and tickers CG/CGAU); https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1854640/000162828026028450/0001628280-26-028450-index.htm (SEC primary filing identifying Centerra Gold Inc.; also hosts the logo graphic).

## Centerstone

- **Exact website:** https://centerstone.org/
- **Plain description:** A national behavioral-health nonprofit providing mental-health, addiction, crisis, primary-care, residential, military and related social-support services, alongside research, education and a foundation.
- **Type / ownership evidence:** Centerstone’s official About page calls it the largest nonprofit behavioral-health organization in the United States; its official footer identifies it as a registered 501(c)(3) and gives EIN 20-0072992. As a nonprofit, no shareholder or private-owner evidence is applicable in the reviewed source.
- **Sources:** https://centerstone.org/about/ (nonprofit description, mission and service scope); https://centerstone.org/ (official homepage and registered 501(c)(3)/EIN footer).
- **Suitable official logo:** https://centerstone.org/wp-content/uploads/centerstone_icon_full_color_lg.svg (Centerstone logo asset on the organization’s official site).
