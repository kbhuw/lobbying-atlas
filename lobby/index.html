About the data

Federal LDA disclosures, 1999–2026. 2026 is a partial year. 0 filings indexed.

Each 2026 filing-name profile has an individual review. “Core facts confirmed” means the reviewed identity and main claims have supporting sources; it does not mean every field or historical alias is verified. Partial reviews have unverified facts, which are explained in the notes. Missing facts remain unknown. Automatic registry matches require a disclosed legal-name match plus city and state against SEC or IRS records. They are not individual research reviews, and do not establish every historical alias or owner. Self-reported descriptions are supplied by LDA filers and matched by exact disclosed name, which does not independently establish identity. “Well-known” is an editorial selection, not a measured ranking or an endorsement. Ownership is dated separately from lobbying activity and may have changed.

Capitalization, punctuation and equivalent legal suffixes are grouped. A bare name joins a suffix variant only when there is one legal-form family. Additional name variants are combined only after an explicit identity review, with the reason and sources shown on the organization page. Original names and filings remain available. Parent companies, subsidiaries and “on behalf of” relationships remain separate unless a specific identity decision establishes otherwise.

Each table row shows the latest posting for a firm and reporting period. Earlier versions remain in the source menu. Reports with no activity and registrations are hidden initially. Conflicting filings with the same latest posting time remain available.

Issue labels summarize available bulk history and can include earlier versions. Spending figures are preserved; do not add company expenses to the income reported by its lobbying firms.
