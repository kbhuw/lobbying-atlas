# Identity 207 — Iridia, Inc.

## Recommendation

Keep the entity as **Iridia, Inc.**, but replace the current description. The existing “energy-storage technology for data centers” wording is unsupported by the original filing and appears to confuse DNA data storage / reduced energy requirements with energy-storage technology.

The official SBIR portfolio establishes the exact legal name, Carlsbad address, UEI, and `iridia.com` bridge. Its 2022 HHS Phase I award was for an accurate, long, fast DNA synthesis device: a semiconductor-based, enzyme-catalyzed DNA synthesizer using nanopores. The current official site now presents Iridia around molecular authentication, CryptoMol molecular identity, Molecular Trust Cloud, SecureShip freight authentication, and aerospace/defense asset traceability. This looks like a product repositioning or expansion by the same entity, not a separate company.

The 2026 Q1 original LDA filing reports the exact client `Iridia, Inc.` and lobbying on FY27 defense appropriations and NDAA requests, but gives no business description or ownership evidence. Ownership remains **Unknown**.

Primary evidence:

- [SBIR company portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/1697307): `IRIDIA, INC.`, 5937 Darwin Ct Ste 109, Carlsbad, CA, UEI `PNJDJMBBR659`, View website `https://www.iridia.com`; award title “Development of an Accurate, Long and Fast DNA Synthesis Device.”
- [SBIR abstract](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/1697307): semiconductor-based DNA synthesizer, enzyme-catalyzed DNA synthesis, nanopore device, and DNA oligonucleotides over 2,000 base pairs.
- [Original LDA disclosure](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/624b3c46-e40f-4cb1-8c4f-1b9e244478be/print/): exact client `Iridia, Inc.`; FY27 defense programmatic appropriations and FY27 NDAA issues.
- [Official Iridia about page](https://iridia.com/about): Southern California manufacturing; leadership spanning molecular science, robotics, nanotechnology, blockchain, cryptography, cybersecurity, and AI; footer `© 2026 Iridia Inc.`
- [Official technology page](https://iridia.com/technology): QAuthR, optical identity, CryptoMol, Molecular Trust Cloud, and SecureShip physical authentication / custody workflow.
- [Official aerospace and defense page](https://iridia.com/industry/aerospace-defense): molecular identities and immutable chain of custody for aerospace components and defense equipment; anti-counterfeit and component authentication use cases.
- [SEC Form D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1708118/000170811820000002/xslFormDX01/primary_doc.xml): Iridia, Inc., Delaware, incorporated 2016, Carlsbad address matching SBIR and official site; industry group Technology.

Suggested replacement description: “Iridia, Inc. develops molecular identity and authentication technology that links physical products, components, and documents to digital records for provenance, anti-counterfeit, and chain-of-custody assurance; its earlier SBIR work involved semiconductor-based DNA synthesis and data-storage technology.”
