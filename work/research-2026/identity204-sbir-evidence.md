# Identity 204 — SBIR evidence

I selected 12 technology, aerospace, defense, or dual-use companies from `reviewed.json`, excluding Darkhive and the heavily reviewed A–D names. Every row has an official SBIR.gov company portfolio page whose body names the company and exposes a `View website` link. Where available, SBIR award pages also expose matching company-domain contact emails. “Confirmed” here means identity/domain bridge only; it does not establish ownership.

- Empirical Systems Aerospace (ES Aero) — [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/192575) names EMPIRICAL SYSTEMS AEROSPACE INC and links `esaero.com`; aerospace/eVTOL, inverter, and swarming-aircraft awards.
- Earth Observant Inc. — [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/1639701) names EARTH OBSERVANT INC and links `eoi.space`; low-flying satellite buses, edge imagery, and Hall-effect thrusters.
- Enpower Inc. — [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/811785) names ENPOWER INC and links `enpowerinc.com`; Li-ion battery manufacturing and integrated ceramic separators.
- Fenix Space, Inc. — [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/1986881) names Fenix Space, Inc. and links `fenixspace.com`; hypersonic target and air-launch platforms.
- FluxWorks — [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/2120789) names FLUXWORKS, INC. and links `fluxworks.co`; magnetic gears for autonomous vehicles, robotics, and lunar systems.
- Empower Equity / Empeq — [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/1658187) names EMPOWER EQUITY INC and links `empeq.co`; machine-learning counterfeit-part identification for military readiness.
- ExoAnalytic Solutions — [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/13002) names EXOANALYTIC SOLUTIONS INC and links `exoanalytic.com`; space-domain awareness, missile-defense simulation, AI, and lasercom.
- Knowmadics — [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/1658973) names KNOWMADICS, INC. and links `knowmadics.com`; RF sensing, AI, and electronic-warfare/security characterization.
- TurbineOne — [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/1703477) names TURBINEONE LLC and links `turbineone.com`; edge AutoML and autonomous-drone perception/targeting.
- Vannevar Labs — [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/1655947) names VANNEVAR LABS INC and links `vannevarlabs.com`; Decrypt OCR/intelligence for Air Force mission planning.
- Titan Dynamics — [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/2452397) names Titan Dynamics Inc and links `titandynamics.org`; sUAS performance-prediction software. The reviewed LDA record has `titandynamics.aero`; retain the SBIR domain mismatch for follow-up.
- X-Bow Launch Systems — [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/1220395) names X-BOW LAUNCH SYSTEMS INC and provides the external website bridge; rocket-motor additive manufacturing and tactical launch systems. Award contacts use `@xbowsystems.com` / `@xbowlaunch.com`.

Ownership is left unknown throughout. The source supports company identity, technology relevance, and the SBIR relationship; it does not support private/public ownership conclusions.
