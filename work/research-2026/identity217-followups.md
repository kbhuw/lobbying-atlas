# Identity 217 follow-ups

## PNI Sensor / Protonex

The exact legal bridge is explicit in PNI Sensor’s own terms: **“Protonex LLC dba PNI Sensor.”** The official SBIR portfolio names `PROTONEX LLC` at 501 American Way, Windsor, CA, links the website, and repeatedly describes the funded technology as PNI Sensor’s FORT and RM3100 products. The LDA row remains an OBO record for `PNI Sensor Corporation` represented by Sbir Advisors. Keep the OBO framing and add the dba bridge; do not infer ownership or rename the client to Protonex.

Sources: [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/2436677), [PNI terms](https://www.pnisensor.com/terms/), [PNI homepage](https://www.pnisensor.com/), [original LDA filing](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/95031904-1248-42d7-bb30-1e29c78a8d66/print/).

## Enexor BioEnergy / Enexor Energy LLC

The official SBIR portfolio’s exact legal awardee is **ENEXOR ENERGY, LLC**, at 1 Enterprise Ct, Franklin, TN, with `www.enexor.com`. The same page’s award abstract calls the business **Enexor BioEnergy (“Enexor”)** and describes its Bio-CHP waste-to-energy systems. Enexor’s own DOE award release repeats the $199,459 award, the same Franklin headquarters address, and the Enexor BioEnergy operating name. A 2021 company release separately uses **Enexor BioEnergy, LLC**. This supports one operating identity and a strong legal-name bridge, but does not prove a formal rename or corporate continuity event. Preserve the distinction in the record and avoid ownership inference. The LDA row is `the Bennett Group DC on Behalf of Enexor Bioenergy`, an OBO filing.

Sources: [SBIR portfolio](https://www.sbir.gov/portfolio/2234831), [Enexor DOE release](https://www.enexor.com/newsroom/pr-doe-biomass-preprocessing), [Enexor Series A release](https://www.enexor.com/newsroom/pr-borgwarner-investment), [original LDA filing](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/e205d3c4-d4be-4793-8dfe-af29141c6425/print/).

## WhiteFox / Next Global Capital

The original filing explicitly says **“Next Global Capital, Inc. OBO WhiteFox Defense Technologies, Inc.”** and identifies drone airspace security as the issue. WhiteFox’s official site identifies **WhiteFox Defense Technologies** and describes counter-drone detection, tracking, identification, and mitigation for military, government, law enforcement, and commercial users. Keep WhiteFox as the represented operating entity and Next Global Capital as the filing/intermediary name. Do not infer that Next Global owns WhiteFox.

Sources: [original LDA filing](https://lda.gov/filings/public/filing/156a1bb7-b668-47f3-b2db-39adf61792b1/print/), [WhiteFox official site](https://www.whitefoxdefense.com/).
